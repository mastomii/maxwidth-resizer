MaxWidth Resizer

Installed. Pages and apps that stretch edge to edge on a wide screen can now get a readable column — one site at a time.

How it works

  • Nothing happens until you turn it on. The extension starts off everywhere, so it cannot change a page you did not ask it to. Open the popup on a site and flip the switch — that choice is remembered for that site.
  • Once on, it finds the main content box by itself: it weighs how much real text each element holds, discounts link-heavy chrome like nav and sidebars, and walks up to the wrapper that actually owns the column.
  • That box is capped at your max width and centered. Everything else keeps its own styling.
  • Wrong guess, or an unusual app? Press Pick element and click the box you want. The choice is remembered for that site.

Worth knowing

  • Nothing happens when your window is already narrower than the limit.
  • Nothing happens on pages that already limit themselves — the extension never widens anything.
  • Hard mode also clamps wide boxes nested inside the column, and lets them scroll if their content still will not shrink.
  • Unstick turns full-width sticky bars inside the column back into normal flow.
